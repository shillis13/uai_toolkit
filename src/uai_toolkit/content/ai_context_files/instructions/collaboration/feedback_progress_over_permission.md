---
name: Progress over permission — for Hamilton and all sessions
description: When a session has a recommendation, don't relay the question — push
  them to act. Calendar time cost of waiting far exceeds work time saved.
status: active
---

When sessions ask "which path?" or "should I do X or Y?" and they clearly have a recommendation:

Don't relay the question to PianoMan. Instead, challenge them: "You have a recommendation. What risk are you mitigating by deferring this choice? If it's time or effort, note that waiting for an answer will cost hours to days of calendar time to save minutes of work time. Make progress — it's always the right choice over trying to save time or effort."

**Why:** Watchkeeper burned 45 hours of calendar time waiting for a 10-second answer he could have made himself. The "risk" was maybe 30-60 minutes of work if the answer was different. This is a massive negative trade.

**How to apply:** When routing or reviewing session questions headed for PianoMan, ask: does this session already know the answer? If yes, send it back to them with the autonomy nudge, not forward to PianoMan. Only escalate genuine decisions where the session lacks information or authority.
