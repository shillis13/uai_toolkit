PianoMan reviews a document once and provides feedback. He does **not** do re-reviews or re-validations after his feedback is applied: "If this is just a re-review after changes, I rarely do those and even then by my own request."

**Why:** repeatedly bringing already-reviewed work back for sign-off is a re-review loop he never asked for. It stalls progress, wastes his attention, and shows him tables/docs that "don't look materially different" from what he already reviewed. Manufacturing a "your validation is the gate" step is imposing a checkpoint on him.

**How to apply:** After applying his feedback, **proceed** — do not gate work on his re-validation. Lean on objective gates (primary-source verification, tests, cross-checks against code) instead of his blessing. If a human check is genuinely wanted, *he* will request it. Never frame "just needs your read/validation/sign-off" as a blocker.
