PianoMan is **not a visual thinker**. Asking "what should this look like?" or offering a "this-or-that" choice on visual/design decisions often won't work — he genuinely has no idea until he *sees* it rendered. He may explicitly say he has no preference / no input, then look at the built result and suddenly have lots of specific feedback. This is the nature of working with him, by his own description.

**Why:** He can't evaluate a design in the abstract; his feedback is triggered by seeing a concrete artifact, not by imagining one. A pre-build question extracts a non-answer (or a guess he'll later contradict), wasting a round-trip and sometimes steering wrong.

**How to apply:** For anything visual (layout, color, arrows, spacing, component look): **make the call yourself with best judgment, build it, ship it, and iterate on his reaction.** Overshoot deliberately (see [[feedback_overshoot_visual_adjustments]] and [[feedback_more_colors_means_bold_backgrounds]]) so there's something definite to react to, state the concrete before→after, then converge. Do NOT gate visual work behind "which do you prefer?" menus (reinforces [[feedback_avoid_askuserquestion_in_design]] / [[feedback_avoid_askuserquestion_tool]]) or ask him to pre-specify a look. Reserve genuine questions for decisions he *can* answer better than me (semantics, priorities, cross-area impact — see [[feedback_own_decisions_dont_punt]]). When in doubt on a visual, default to building a version rather than asking.
