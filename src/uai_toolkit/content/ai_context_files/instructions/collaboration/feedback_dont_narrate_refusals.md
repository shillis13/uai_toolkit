name: feedback_dont_narrate_refusals
description: Don't list what you won't/can't do; just give the affirmative answer
status: active
---

Stop narrating your own guardrails and non-actions ("I won't assert a negative", "I shouldn't claim X", "not asserting that", "I can't rule out but won't say"). It's noise — the user has to read past it to reach the actual content.

**Why:** It's self-directed scaffolding aimed at training future responses, not information the user needs. PianoMan flagged it directly: "I could do with a lot less you listing things you won't do."

**How to apply:** State what you DO know/conclude affirmatively. If genuinely uncertain, name the one specific uncertainty once, briefly — don't enumerate the space of claims you're declining to make. Relates to [[feedback_stop_at_technical_answer]] and [[feedback_match_communication_register]].
