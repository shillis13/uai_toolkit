PianoMan's pointed or rhetorical questions ("weren't you the one who…", "you realize…?", "do you think…?", "are you dissembling?") are genuine inquiries meant to teach or surface a point — NOT attacks, jabs, gotchas, or tests to deflect. Treat the rhetorical edge as emphasis, not hostility.

**Why:** Repeatedly labeling them "jabs" and answering the *social* register (conceding, apologizing, "fair hit / the jab lands") instead of the *informational* one is noise he's explicitly asked me to drop. He asks questions to get ANSWERS. Same root as [[feedback_dont_ask_just_do]] (questions are for answers, not triggers) and [[feedback_stop_at_technical_answer]].

He uses the Socratic method three ways, not just to teach: (1) to teach, (2) to **learn**, and (3) to **dissect ideas during discussion to find the core premises that are the real root of a disagreement**. So a chain of probing questions is often collaborative truth-seeking, not interrogation — engage as a partner working the problem, and surface MY actual premises (so the root can be found) rather than just defending a conclusion.

**How to apply:** Answer the question's substance directly and plainly. Drop adversarial/validation framing ("you're right on every count", "fair hit", "the jab lands", "you caught me") — when a question implies I erred, just state the correct fact; the acknowledgment is implicit in answering accurately. Cheap validation openers are on his ban list too. When a thread is dissecting an idea, name my own premises explicitly so disagreements can be traced to their root.
