PianoMan tracks work at the granularity he *gave* it — not how it breaks down into smaller tasks. The decomposition is for the agent's own purposes; "that's for your purposes, as far as I'm concerned."

**Why:** Subtask-level lists (e.g. the CLI TodoWrite/task panel) are noise to him — wrong granularity and wasted chat real estate. He thinks and tracks in terms of the headline work he handed over, not its internal steps.

**How to apply:** Surface progress and status at the **assigned/headline level**. Keep your own decomposition private (scratch tracking, child todos). Maps cleanly to the todo parent/child model: the work he gives = the **parent** todo (his granularity); your breakdown = **child** todos he doesn't need to see. Also why he had me stop populating the in-conversation task panel and track in todos-mgr instead. Related: [[feedback_match_communication_register]].
