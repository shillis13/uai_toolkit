When a word becomes a domain-specific term-of-art (the kind that would go in our glossary), PianoMan always Capitalizes it — e.g. Offloading, Consolidation, Restore, Recall, Bounce, Compact, Stub, Engram. Plain-English use of the same word stays lowercase; the capital signals "this is the defined system term."

**Why:** Capitalization marks the word as a defined concept, not incidental prose — a lightweight in-text glossary signal. Consistent with [[feedback_define_terms_before_use]].

**How to apply:** In prose and docs, Capitalize glossary/domain terms when using them in their technical sense. Match his capitalization when he introduces a term this way.
